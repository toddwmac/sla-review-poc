USU / AGI Service Level Agreement Review

Executive Assessment

The agreement is fundamentally well designed and appears intended to demonstrate institutional independence while preserving operational efficiency through AGI shared services.

The document effectively protects academic authority, accreditation responsibilities, and Board governance. Most remaining risks relate to operational precision rather than structural deficiencies.

Primary concerns identified:

  • Undefined approval authority terminology
  • Incomplete service-level metrics
  • Ambiguous change-control requirements
  • Insufficiently detailed data-transition obligations
  • Lack of escalation timing requirements
  • Limited vendor governance requirements

Recommendations

HIGH

1. Formal Authority Matrix

SLA Sections: Definitions, Governance & Monitoring, Service Matrices

Observation

Terms such as Approve, Validate, Review, Authorize, Monitor, and Oversee are used extensively but lack formal definitions.

Recommendation

Create a Decision Rights Appendix that explicitly defines each action and assigns authority.

Proposed Enhancement

Introduce a governance appendix where every service area contains Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed parties together with approval requirements.

HIGH

2. Service Level Metrics Appendix

SLA Sections: Performance Standards

Metrics exist but enforceable targets are deferred.

Add mandatory targets, owner, formula, reporting frequency, measurement source, tolerance band, and escalation point.

HIGH

3. Material Change Definition

SLA Sections: MIS, Monitoring, Governance

The term "material change" appears repeatedly but remains undefined.

Define technology, finance, outsourcing, compliance, and student-facing changes requiring institutional approval.

HIGH

4. Data Ownership & Exit Rights

SLA Sections: MIS, Contracts and Transition

Provide mandatory export formats, transition timelines, assistance obligations, conversion support, validation testing, and destruction certification requirements.

MEDIUM

5. Vendor Governance Program

Require annual vendor assessments, risk categorization, continuity reviews, contract ownership, and dependency analysis.

MEDIUM

6. Employer of Record Schedule

Maintain an authoritative workforce appendix clearly listing employment relationships and authority boundaries.

MEDIUM

7. Escalation Timelines

Specify maximum escalation windows for compliance, security, accreditation, and financial events.

LOW

8. RACI Summary Appendix

Produce a one-page accountability map for trustees, executives, auditors, and accreditors.

LOW

9. System Inventory Registry

Define all system names including CNS and identify system owners and authoritative records.

LOW

10. Accreditation Protection Clause

When institutional obligations conflict with efficiency, institutional and accreditation requirements prevail.

Authority Matrix Enhancement

Recommended new appendix defining: Approve, Validate, Review, Authorize, Monitor, Oversee, Consult, Recommend and Notify.

Performance Standards Enhancement

Each metric should include: Target, Formula, Owner, Reporting Frequency, Exception Criteria and Escalation Trigger.

Change Control Enhancement

Define categories requiring University approval.

Data Governance Enhancement

Add export, retention, recovery, and exit requirements.

Vendor Governance Enhancement

Establish annual vendor review procedures.

Employment Governance Enhancement

Clarify legal employer assignment for all workforce categories.
