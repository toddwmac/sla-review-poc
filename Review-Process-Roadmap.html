6MOS ↔ v2 traceability
The workflow in brief SLA v2 — the only document used — was assessed independently by four AI models, producing 80 findings; each assessment also shipped as an interactive, reviewer-ready document. The four assessments then went to three different models, each comparing all four for alignment and disagreement — twelve lenses in total (4 × 3). A fusion engine condensed the assessments and the comparison reports into a single final report, and the original MOS feedback on SLA v1 was compared against v2 and the model assessments to see what had been addressed. No human reviewed any AI output in this run — the only human input was the original MOS feedback — and the built-in reviewer-commenting features, though present, were not exercised. Four independent AI assessments of Draft 2.0 in this repository

Nov 7, 2026

Four AI systems reviewed Draft 2.0 separately, without seeing each other’s findings, each flagging areas that could be improved — contractual machinery, legal drafting, operational clarity, and accreditation readiness. Together: 80 findings. Each assessment also shipped as an interactive, reviewer-ready document — built for distribution to human reviewers, though none were distributed or annotated in this run.

4

Cross-model consistency assessment in this repository

Nov 20, 2026

Three different models — GPT, Gemini Pro, and Claude Haiku — each read all four assessments and grouped the 80 findings into themes and topics. This is the consistency check: where several models converge, the theme is strong signal; where they conflict, the disagreement is named; where only one reviewer raised an item, it is marked as a single-reviewer issue worth a look.

Consumed
The four AI assessments (80 items)
Produced
GPT analysisHTML · Gemini Pro analysisHTML · Claude Haiku analysisHTML — three independent comparison reports (PDF and MD alternates in the inventory)
The three comparison reports went into a fusion engine to be reconciled
5

Fusion engine → one summary view in this repository

Nov 20, 2026

The comparison models critiqued each other’s summaries and reconciled the differences — catching real errors along the way (one analysis had labeled disputed details “unanimous”; the panel corrected it and recorded why). The corrected material was fused into a single board-facing summary.

Consumed
The three comparison reports
Produced
Fusion engine transcriptPDF (audit trail) → Final Comparative Board BriefHTML — the pipeline’s summary deliverable. Recommendation: endorse the framework in principle; complete the pre-signature worklist before signing.
With the AI side complete, the loop closes back at the original MOS feedback
6

MOS feedback ↔ v2 traceability in this repository

Nov 20, 2026

The closing loop: every one of the 19 MOS comments is checked against what Draft 2.0 actually says, and cross-referenced to the AI findings that re-flag the same point. Result: 10 closed, 3 closed with residuals, 4 framework-closed but deferred, 1 partial, 1 not assessable — and confirmation that the remaining AI findings are mostly new ground beyond the MOS scope.

Consumed
MOS comments + Draft 2.0 + the four AI assessments
