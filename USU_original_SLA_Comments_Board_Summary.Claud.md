# USU-AGI Service Level Agreement: Critical Comments Summary
## Board-Level Review (Draft 09.12.2026, MOS Edits)

---

## GOVERNANCE & INSTITUTIONAL CONTROL ISSUES (Critical)

### 1. **Fundamental Responsibility Contradiction** ⚠️ CRITICAL
**Comment MOS1 (Page 4) – President's Letter**
- **Issue:** The draft contains contradictory statements about USU's responsibility for quality and integrity
  - States: "it bears no responsibility for ensuring the quality and integrity of all activities conducted in its name"
  - Later states: "AGI may perform specific services...USU retains final responsibility for ensuring the quality, integrity, compliance, and effectiveness"
- **Board Impact:** This creates legal and accreditation risk. USU's fiduciary board duty requires institutional control over all activities in its name.
- **Action Required:** Clarify and reconcile language to reflect that **USU retains final responsibility** regardless of AGI service delivery

### 2. **USU Board Governance & Oversight** ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT
**Comment MOS4 (Page 7) – Board of Trustees Authority**
- **Current Gap:** Governance section under-specifies the Board's authority over matters reserved to the University
- **Required Addition:** Statement that "The USU Board of Trustees retains governing and fiduciary oversight of the University in accordance with applicable law, accreditation requirements, and University governing documents...and retains authority over matters reserved to the University"
- **Includes:** Academic programs, curriculum, faculty oversight, admissions, student appeals, accreditation submissions

### 3. **AGI Board Boundaries** ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT
**Comment MOS5 (Page 7) – AGI Board of Directors**
- **Issue:** AGI Board authority scope unclear; could be confused with University authority
- **Required Clarification:** "The AGI Board of Directors oversees AGI's corporate activities and AGI's performance of obligations under this Agreement. **The AGI Board does not exercise the University's reserved academic or institutional authority.**"

### 4. **Definition of Reserved University Functions** 🔴 CRITICAL FOR ACCREDITATION
**Comment MOS3 (Page 6) – Terms Definition**
- **Issue:** Document fails to explicitly list areas where USU exercises authority (required by WSCUC)
- **Required Additions:** USU must explicitly retain responsibility for:
  - Academic programs and curriculum
  - Admissions standards and decisions
  - Faculty appointment, oversight, and evaluation
  - Instruction, grading, and student learning assessment
  - Program review and degree conferral
  - Student complaints, appeals, and institutional policies
  - Accreditation submissions and representations

## ACADEMIC QUALITY & ACCREDITATION CONTROL (Critical)

### 5. **Curriculum Management – Faculty Control** 🔴 CRITICAL
**Comment MOS13 (Page 24) – Academic Operations**
- **Issue:** Language "AGI manages curriculum" gives AGI inappropriate academic control
- **Accreditation Risk:** Faculty and university must control curriculum development and approval
- **Required Correction:** Replace with "AGI provides authorized administrative, production, platform, and technical support" for curriculum
- **Also:** Comment MOS14 flags this as problematic for accreditation—faculty must have control over curriculum

### 6. **Syllabi Development & Approval**
**Comment MOS13 (Page 24)**
- **Current:** Unclear who develops and approves syllabi
- **Required:** "USU develops and approves syllabi; AGI may support formatting, publication, distribution, and system configuration"

### 7. **Faculty Authority Over Instruction & Evaluation**
**Comment MOS13 (Page 24) – Faculty Development**
- **Issue:** Not clear that USU (faculty) control academic priorities and content
- **Required:** "USU establishes academic priorities and content; AGI may provide logistics, platforms, or approved programming support"

### 8. **Academic Records – University Reservation**
**Comment MOS12 (Page 22) – Academic Support/Student Services**
- **Issue:** Confusion about who controls degree audits, degree conferral, credit recording
- **Required:** Reserve to USU: "awarding and recording credit, degree audits, degree conferral, and official academic records"

## FINANCIAL & ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL (Significant)

### 9. **Financial Authority & Approval** ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT
**Comment MOS10 (Page 17) – Business Office Support**
- **Missing Clarifications:**
  - Budget approval authority and thresholds
  - Bank signatory authority
  - Material commitments approval requirements
  - University access to financial records and audit
- **Required Addition:** "University maintains independent audit access and timely access to legal and financial information affecting institutional operations"

### 10. **Shared Cost Allocation & Governance**
**Comment MOS10 (Page 17)**
- **Issue:** No methodology specified for intercompany cost allocation
- **Required:** "Shared costs require documented allocation methodology, periodic University review, and approval of intercompany cost agreements"

### 11. **Technology Data Governance** ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT
**Comment MOS9 (Page 15) – Technology Services**
- **Missing:** Clarification that institutional data remain available to USU
- **Required Additions:**
  - University approval of product selection, access standards, priorities, material system changes
  - Role-based access controls and incident response procedures
  - Data ownership, portability, return, and destruction responsibilities
  - Backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity requirements

## REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & INSTITUTIONAL CONTROL

### 12. **Regulatory Submissions & Accreditation** ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT
**Comment MOS15 (Page 26) – Regulatory Compliance**
- **Issue:** Unclear who controls institutional compliance submissions and corrective actions
- **Required Additions:**
  - "Add USU approval of policies, submissions, accreditation narratives, public disclosures, and corrective actions"
  - "Clarify University designation and oversight of responsible institutional officials and final authority over complaints and grievances"
  - "Require source-data access, validation, change control, reporting calendars, and documentation supporting submissions"

### 13. **Financial Aid Compliance & Oversight**
**Comment MOS8 (Page 13) – Student Finance and Financial Aid**
- **Issue:** Responsibility gaps on policy approval and institutional oversight
- **Required:** "Fill USU cells with policy approval, institutional eligibility oversight, audit and program-review review, report approval, and corrective-action approval"
- **Accountability Measures Needed:** Processing timeliness, accuracy, unresolved file aging, audit findings, complaint trends, and corrective-action status
